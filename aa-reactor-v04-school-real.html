We stopped putting chemists out. Let's fix it.

In the US, hands-on chemistry has been cut from many schools due to liability, cost, and fear. Kids never see a precipitate form, never watch CO2 bubble, never feel a beaker get warm from an exothermic reaction.

This app is the safe return: real elements, real concepts (valence, exothermic/endothermic, gas evolution, precipitate, polarity, conservation of mass), but ONLY with reactions from middle-school textbooks, flagged by safety.

GREEN - Safe supervised hands-on with goggles

Dilute acid + base → warm beaker, neutralization
RED - Conceptual only, do not attempt
Combustion equations shown for energy, not how-to

Visuals: bubbles = gas evolution, white solid settling = precipitate, orange glow = exothermic. No explosives, no illicit drugs, no instructions for dangerous synthesis. All content is standard curriculum.
