Teacher Pack — 5 Lessons

Mission: Bring hands-on chemistry back safely. Real elements + safe reactions only. GREEN safe with goggles, YELLOW teacher demo, RED conceptual only.

Lesson 1: Gas Evolution — Vinegar + Baking Soda GREEN

NaHCO3 + CH3COOH → CO2 + H2O + NaCH3COO — bubbles, conservation.

Lesson 2: Exothermic Neutralization YELLOW

HCl 0.1M + NaOH 0.1M → NaCl + H2O + heat -57kJ/mol — warm beaker, teacher handles dilute.

Lesson 3: Precipitate GREEN

Clear → white solid settles — crystal growth visual.

Lesson 4: Rusting Slow Oxidation GREEN

4Fe + 3O2 + 6H2O → Fe2O3 — slow exothermic, same as burning but slow.

Lesson 5: VSEPR Builder

H2O bent 104.5° (O 2 lone pairs), CO2 linear 180°, CH4 tetrahedral 109.5°, NH3 trigonal pyramidal — valence + lone pairs = shape.

Safety: goggles, gloves, ventilation, 0.1M dilute max, no concentrated, no explosives/illicit. App simulates only.
